# "If I Had a Hundred-Fifty Points" — Wizards League Roast (Week 4 Power Rankings)

A parody of Carolina MiC's *If I Had $150,000*, ranking the league worst to best.

**Written to sound like Tuesday.** The song will be recorded Monday, Oct 5, before *Monday Night Football* (ATL @ NO), and delivered later in the week. So the lyrics:
- call this a **power ranking**, not the official standings, which aren't final until MNF;
- mention only results that were already locked before MNF;
- never mention the three matchups that depended on MNF (Leeroy–Kyle, Brandon–Luke, Jarrod–Craig);
- state no current records, no points-for leaders, and no exact scores for any team with a player in the MNF game. Pollitto's final score depends on Alvin Kamara, so the lyrics only cite Jared's 103.

## Production notes

### Beat
- **`beat.wav` / `beat.mid`:** an original retro-funk instrumental built by `make_beat.py`. It runs 104 BPM in F major: slap bass, e-piano stabs, synth brass, handclaps. The feel is in the same lane as the original, but the groove is written from scratch. It's 88 bars (~3:23), laid out two bars per lyric line (four bars per person), with a brass hit on the first bar of each verse for the rank reveal. The WAV is a rough synth preview. For a fuller sound, open `beat.mid` in GarageBand and swap in real instruments. `beat_preview.wav` is the first 55 seconds.
- **`beat_pfunk.wav` / `beat_pfunk.mid` (v2, closer feel):** built by `make_beat_pfunk.py` at the reference song's 109 BPM, in a P-Funk / Earth, Wind & Fire style: driving synth bass, clavinet, a synth lead on the hooks, and horn stabs. It follows the same lyric layout (~3:14). No audio from the original was sampled or transcribed.
- The original instrumental belongs to Carolina MiC. If you want his actual beat, reach out through [Bandcamp](https://carolinamic.bandcamp.com/) or [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/carolinamic).

### Vocals
- **AI vocal:** see `SUNO.md` for a paste-ready style box, exclude list, and lyrics split into two parts. It describes a generic smooth, playful funk-crooner style rather than cloning his voice.
- **Your own vocal:** record over `beat.wav`. It's still the funniest option for the league.

### Video: living-room shoot (one afternoon, one phone)
1. **Setup:** Phone on a tripod, landscape, wide shot of the couch and TV. Leave the Sleeper standings or the NFL RedZone on the TV behind you.
2. **Base take:** Film yourself dancing to the full song 3–4 times. Do a goofy, full-commitment two-step: side-to-side bounce, shoulder shimmy, finger points at the camera on each rank number. Corny is the whole point.
3. **Per-verse cutaways (3–5 sec each), props:**
   - **Jon:** Giants/Titans hats stacked on your head, sad trombone face.
   - **Jarrod:** Beer in one hand, putter in the other, laptop open on a Teams call. Chicken-dance on "Chicken Little."
   - **Leeroy:** Paper crown falling off your head, "COMMISH" sign.
   - **Luke:** Bourbon bottle labeled **DEMON WATER**, then push a lawnmower past the window at "noon on a Tuesday."
   - **Ben:** Soccer ball, whistle, exaggerated flop onto the couch, pull out a red card. Balloon or oversized hat for the head bit.
   - **Brandon:** Purple Vikings horns, laptop with a fake "AI-generated update" on screen, Favre jersey if anyone owns one.
   - **Kyle:** Clock reading 4:25, jump up cheering, then freeze and slowly sit.
   - **Pollitto:** Phone with an AMC chart, toy rocket launched off the coffee table.
   - **Craig:** Laptop showing "degen.ai," throwing fake money around.

   - **Jared:** Line up your 4 trophies or rings on the coffee table and slow-mo point at each one.
